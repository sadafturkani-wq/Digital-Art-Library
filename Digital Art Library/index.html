Our Craft

A gallery built like a museum,
delivered like a website.

Every work in Lumière is scanned or reconstructed from archival sources, colour-calibrated against the original pigments, and re-rendered for screens we trust. Wall labels carry real scholarship; lighting is virtual, but the connoisseurship is not.

All images in the collection are public-domain works, presented with credit to their holding institutions and to Wikimedia Commons.
